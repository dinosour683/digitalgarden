# Cloudblot, 1st of Lisdron 4999

As of today, I've decided to begin writing my entries in Common instead of Druidic, as I fear that I might not be alive much longer and someone else may have to start reading my journal. 

Over the past few days, unusual events have been occurring in this part of the forest. The plants in my garden are dying and going black, and animals seem to be feral. I'm worried that this may be another case of The Infection. 

To the best of my knowledge, there hasn't been a case of The Infection in around half a century, so I'm not sure how it has returned. Additionally, the infection appears to be slightly different from usual. It seems to spread faster, and through killing. Yesterday I saw a baby deer die from an infected eagle, and a few hours later the deer was alive again and infected. 

I have spent most of my recent days investigating this infection sprouting up locally.
